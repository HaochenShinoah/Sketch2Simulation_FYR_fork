CONFIGURATION INSTRUCTIONS - Aspen HYSYS COM, Step 6 (simple_v5, revised 2026-10-22)
===================================================================================
Recreated for the Sketch2Simulation fork (the original file is proprietary and
was not released). Every port member and column call below was verified live on
Aspen HYSYS V11 on 2026-10-22 (docs/HYSYS_COM_FACTS.md). Audience: the
Configuration Agent (a code model) and the Fixer. Read every section before
writing code.

0. WHAT YOU ARE DOING
---------------------
You attach every material stream listed in STRUCTURED_JSON.units_unit_view to the
inlet and outlet ports of the HYSYS unit operations that Step 4 created, by
filling the block
    # === Step 6: Connect Streams (Agent 3) ===
Use only the helpers already defined in the script (Step 3b):
    connect(unit_key, stream_key, role)                      ordinary units; roles in sections 2-3
    connect_column_feed(unit_key, stream_key)                columns, every stream in streams_in
        ... optional: stage=<n> (tray number), position="top" | "bottom" (section 4.1)
    connect_column_product(unit_key, stream_key, role)       columns; role in section 4.2
        ... optional: stage=<n> for "side_draw"
    connect_energy(unit_key)                                 energy stream of a reactor (section 4.4)
    skip(unit_key, stream_key, reason)                       a listed stream that has no free port
                                                             on a NON-column unit (section 1.5)
Every helper reads the port back from HYSYS after attaching: a call that HYSYS
accepts without attaching the stream is reported as a failure at the end of the run,
so choose the role that really fits the unit.
Role names denote HYSYS ports, not physical phases: "vapour_out" is the top outlet
(VapourProduct) and "liquid_out" the bottom outlet (LiquidProduct) of a vessel. A 2-phase
separator therefore sends its lighter (overhead) liquid through "vapour_out".
unit_key / stream_key are the "unit_id" / "id" strings of the JSON (the keys of the
units / streams dictionaries). The HYSYS category of each unit is visible in Step 4:
add_unit(key, name, TYPES["<category>"]).

1. OUTPUT CONTRACT
------------------
1.1 Return only the Step 6 block as plain text: no ``` fences, no language tag, no prose
    before or after, and do not repeat Steps 1-5 or 7 (the pipeline splices your block into
    the script shown above). The block starts with the exact line
    # === Step 6: Connect Streams (Agent 3) ===
    and ends right before   # === Step 7: Save Simulation ===   (do not write that line;
    Step 7 is owned by the template). Do not keep the two placeholder comment lines of the
    current Step 6 block.
1.2 Group the connections per unit, in the order of units_unit_view. Each group starts with
        # --- <unit_id> (<unit_name>) ---
    (unit_name copied verbatim, even when it contains parentheses itself).
    A unit without streams gets only this header line.
1.3 Inside a group: all streams_in first, then all streams_out, in JSON order.
1.4 Every listed stream is connected exactly once per unit, and nothing that is not listed.
    Do not invent product or feed streams a HYSYS unit "would need" (for example the
    missing vapour product of a reactor with a single effluent): HYSYS only reports such
    gaps when solving, and this script builds the case without solving it.
    An intermediate stream appears in streams_out of its source unit and in streams_in of
    its destination: connect it at both ends (one line in each group). A feed stream
    (from_unit null) is connected only at its to_unit; a product stream (to_unit null)
    only at its from_unit.
1.5 skip(unit_key, stream_key, reason) is accepted only on non-column units and only with
    one of these three reasons, quoted verbatim:
        "single-port unit, no free port"      a second inlet/outlet of a pump, valve, ...
        "no free vessel port"                 a third outlet of a 2-phase vessel, etc.
        "single burner feed"                  a second burner-side stream of a fired heater
    Any other reason, and any skip on a column, is reported as a failure.
1.6 Forbidden: creating units or streams, setting specifications, energy streams other
    than connect_energy, imports, Dispatch, loops over the JSON, redefining helpers,
    try/except, SaveAs, calling finish_connections() yourself, touching Steps 1-5 or 7.
1.7 Short comments are welcome (why a role was chosen).

2. ROLE BY UNIT CATEGORY  (role for each streams_in | role for each streams_out)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    pump, compressor, expander, valve, heater, cooler, air_cooler, recycle, pipe
        "single_in" | "single_out"
        Exactly one of each (the normaliser inserts a MIXER_TO_* mixer or a TEE_FROM_* tee
        when a sketch sends several streams into or out of such a unit). If the JSON still
        lists more, connect the first and write
            skip("<unit_id>", "<stream id>", "single-port unit, no free port")
        (a second "single_in" on the same unit would silently replace the first; the
        end-of-run check reports that as a failure).
    mixer
        "multi_in" for every inlet | "mixer_out" for the single outlet
    tee (including TEE_FROM_* units inserted by the normaliser)
        "tee_in" for the single inlet | "tee_out" for every outlet
    separator (2-phase), tank, reactor_conversion, reactor_equilibrium, reactor_gibbs,
    reactor_cstr
        "multi_in" for every inlet (the connect_point of an inlet is irrelevant) | outlets by
        connect_point, section 3: only "vapour_out" and "liquid_out" exist on these units
        (a tank with a single outlet: "liquid_out" whatever its connect_point; a second
        tank outlet named vent / vapour / gas: "vapour_out").
        "heavy_liquid_out" on a 2-phase separator attaches nothing (HYSYS accepts the call
        silently); it is reported as a failure. Use it only on separator_3phase.
        (reactor_cstr: VapourProduct / LiquidProduct exist in the HYSYS object model but
        were not exercised live.)
    reactor_pfr
        "multi_in" for every inlet | "pfr_out" for its single effluent (the plug-flow
        reactor has one Product port and no vapour/liquid ports; member from the HYSYS
        object model, not exercised live)
    separator_3phase
        "multi_in" | overhead -> "vapour_out"; of the two liquid outlets, the one named
        water / aqueous / brine / caustic / heavy / drawn lowest -> "heavy_liquid_out",
        the other (hydrocarbon, oil, light) -> "light_liquid_out"
    heat_exchanger
        pair each outlet with the inlet whose name it continues ("Reactor effluent"
        -> "Cooled reactor effluent"); the inlet "Mixer outlet to <unit>" that the
        normaliser made is the process side and pairs with the remaining outlet;
        with no name match, pair inlet i with outlet i (same position in the lists).
        pair 1 -> "tube_in" / "tube_out", pair 2 -> "shell_in" / "shell_out".
        A single pair (only the process side is drawn) uses the tube side only.
    component_splitter
        "splitter_in" | overhead -> "splitter_overhead", bottom -> "splitter_bottom"
    fired_heater
        every process inlet -> "fired_in", every process outlet -> "fired_out"
        (HYSYS accepts several radiant tube passes, so never skip a process feed; the
        single mixer before a furnace, when wanted, is inserted upstream by the normaliser)
        a stream named flue gas / stack gas / combustion products -> "flue_gas_out"
        the burner-side stream -> "fuel_in" (the single BurnerFeed port): the fuel /
        fuel gas / natural gas / fuel oil stream, the (combustion) air when no fuel
        stream is drawn, or the
        "Burner feed to <unit>" outlet of the burner mixer MIXER_TO_<unit>_BURNER that
        the normaliser inserts when fuel and air are both drawn
        a second burner-side stream that still reaches the fired heater directly (air
        next to a fuel stream, no burner mixer) has no port of its own:
            skip("<unit_id>", "<stream id>", "single burner feed")
    column_distillation, column_absorber, column_reboiled_absorber, column_refluxed_absorber,
    column_3phase, column_extractor
        section 4 (never use connect() on a column; the helper refuses it)

3. OUTLET ROLE FROM connect_point (separators, tanks, reactors)
----------------------------------------------------------------
    overhead         -> "vapour_out"   (also for a liquid-named overhead stream such as
                        "Desalted crude oil" leaving the top of a 2-phase desalter: the
                        vessel has only a top and a bottom port; say so in a comment)
    bottom           -> "liquid_out"
    side / unknown   -> "vapour_out" if the stream name contains gas, vapour, vapor, vent,
                        off-gas, fuel gas or overhead; otherwise "liquid_out"
    A port is used once per unit. If two outlets map to the same role, give the second the
    other role (vapour_out <-> liquid_out) when it is still free and say so in a comment.
    If a unit that is not a 3-phase separator has more outlets than free ports (e.g. two
    overhead gas outlets on a 2-phase separator), connect the best matches and write
        skip("<unit_id>", "<stream id>", "no free vessel port")
    for the rest, so the summary lists it.
    Inlets: separators, tanks and reactors accept any number of inlets (multi_in); never
    add a mixer.
    Reactors: a reactor template has the same two ports (vapour_out = top, liquid_out =
    bottom). The phase of the reaction decides before the drawing does: a single
    effluent of a gas-phase reaction (hydrogen co-feed, cracking, dealkylation,
    reforming, oxidation, combustion, or a gas-like stream name) is "vapour_out" even when
    it is drawn at the bottom; a single effluent of a liquid-phase reaction is
    "liquid_out" even when drawn at the top or side; say so in a comment. Two effluents
    follow connect_point as for separators.
    A stream in streams_in that carries integrated_via (e.g. recycle toluene coming from
    an absorbed reboiler) is an ordinary inlet of the current unit: use the unit's inlet
    role; integrated_via only documents where it came from.

4. COLUMNS
----------
4.1 Feeds: one line per stream in streams_in
        connect_column_feed("<unit_id>", "<stream id>")
    places the feed on a tray (the middle stage unless stage=<n> is given; use stage=<n>
    when the unit view entry carries "stage" or the sketch states it). The tray section
    exports its own main-flowsheet stream, which takes over the name and the upstream
    connection of the placeholder created in Step 5, so nothing else changes.
    Special cases:
      - a reflux return that still enters the column from a drum or pump that was NOT
        folded (it is listed in streams_in):     connect_column_feed(..., stage=1)
      - column_absorber / column_extractor: the lean liquid / solvent / absorbent / light
        liquid feed -> position="top"; the rich gas / vapour / heavy liquid feed ->
        position="bottom" (these are the top-stage liquid and bottom-stage vapour feeds
        of the template; no tray number is needed)
      - a stream that LEAVES another column (its from_unit is a unit created with
        TYPES["column_*"] in Step 4; a side draw or product of column A that feeds
        column B): position="top" for a liquid, position="bottom" for a gas; such a
        stream cannot be placed on a tray, and the helper refuses to export it twice.
    A stage outside 1..NumberOfStages (10 on the default template) is clamped and noted.
4.2 Products: one line per stream in streams_out
        connect_column_product("<unit_id>", "<stream id>", "<role>")
    Choose the role in this order of precedence:
      a) integrated_via is set on the stream (it left an absorbed condenser / reflux drum /
         reflux pump / reboiler; see hysys_overrides of the unit). integrated_via always
         beats connect_point (a "side" stream drawn from the reflux drum is still a
         distillate):
            via the reboiler                                             -> "bottoms"
            via the condenser / overhead system, gas-like name
              (gas, vapour, vapor, vent, off-gas, fuel gas, purge)       -> "overhead_vapour"
            via the condenser / overhead system, water-like name
              (water, aqueous, sour water, waste water, brine)           -> "water_draw"
            via the condenser / overhead system, otherwise               -> "distillate"
      b) connect_point overhead, gas-like name (gas, vapour, vapor, vent, off-gas, fuel gas,
         purge, overhead gas)                                    -> "overhead_vapour"
      c) connect_point overhead, otherwise (distillate, naphtha, product, light ends),
         or the only overhead stream of the column                -> "distillate"
      d) connect_point bottom, water-like name (water, aqueous, sour water, waste water)
         AND the column has another bottom stream (or the stream carries integrated_via
         of the condenser)                                        -> "water_draw"
         (a lone water bottoms product, e.g. of a sour-water stripper, is "bottoms")
      e) connect_point bottom (residue, bottoms, heavy, recycle, any other)
                                                                 -> "bottoms"
      f) connect_point side (kero, diesel, AGO, side cut, pumparound draw)
                                                                 -> "side_draw"
    Per column type: column_absorber and column_reboiled_absorber have no condenser:
    their overhead stream is always "overhead_vapour" and their bottom "bottoms" (never
    distillate / water_draw). column_extractor: light liquid product -> "distillate",
    heavy liquid product -> "bottoms", no overhead_vapour. column_refluxed_absorber:
    overhead_vapour / distillate / bottoms as for a distillation column (its product
    members were not exercised live).
    Each of overhead_vapour, distillate, water_draw and bottoms is used at most once per
    column. A second stream for one of these roles becomes a side draw:
      - a second gas-like overhead:  connect_column_product(..., "side_draw", stage=1,
                                     phase="vapour")
      - a second bottom product:     connect_column_product(..., "side_draw", stage=10)
        (the template has 10 stages; these two placements were not exercised live)
      - any other duplicate:         "side_draw" without a stage.
    "side_draw" may be used for any number of streams (one line per side cut). The helper
    creates a real draw on the tray section and exports it under the stream's name.
    The phase follows the name (a gas-like name -> vapour draw, a water-like name ->
    water draw, anything else incl. "gas oil" / "gasoline" -> liquid draw); state it
    explicitly with phase="liquid" | "vapour" | "water" whenever the name is ambiguous.
    Add stage=<n> when the unit view entry carries "stage" or the sketch shows the tray;
    without it the draws are placed on stages 2, 4, 6, 8 (a fifth draw needs stage=).
    "water_draw" switches the condenser to three-phase; use it only for a water stream
    that really leaves the condenser / reflux drum.
4.3 Never connect reflux, reboiler-return or other internal streams of the overhead or
    reboiler system: the normaliser removed them, and the template column already
    contains its condenser and reboiler. All external streams of the absorbed equipment
    are connected to the column itself.
    When a reflux drum or reflux pump is still a unit of its own, its streams are ordinary
    streams: the drum is a separator, the pump a pump, and the reflux return that enters
    the column is a feed on the top stage (4.1). It is listed in streams_in, so it is
    connected, never skipped.
4.4 Reactor energy streams: after the streams of every reactor_* unit, add one line
        connect_energy("<unit_id>")
    (the paper's flowsheets show an energy stream "E" at each reactor). Nothing else gets
    an energy stream. connect_energy lines are not stream connections: they are not
    counted in the self-check and need no entry in units_unit_view.
4.5 skip() is never used on a column; a column stream without a matching role is a
    "side_draw".

5. HYSYS COM FACTS BEHIND THE HELPERS (reference only; never copy into your output)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    All verified live on Aspen HYSYS V11 (2026-10-22). Every attachment is read back from
    op.AttachedFeeds.Names / op.AttachedProducts.Names; a member that HYSYS accepts without
    attaching counts as a failure, and the end of the run re-checks every attachment.
    single-port operations (pump, valve, compressor, expander, heater, cooler, air cooler,
        recycle, pipe): op.FeedStream / op.ProductStream = <stream object>
        (assigning a second stream replaces the first)
    mixer:            Feeds.Add(stream) and Product        tee: FeedStream and Products.Add(stream)
    2-phase separator (flashtank), tank, conversion / equilibrium / Gibbs reactors:
        Feeds.Add(stream); products VapourProduct / LiquidProduct (HeavyLiquidProduct is
        accepted but attaches nothing on these); CSTR (kineticreactorop) has the same
        members in the object model; PFR (pfreactorop): Feeds.Add and Product
    3-phase separator (sep3op): Feeds.Add; VapourProduct, LiquidProduct (= light liquid),
        HeavyLiquidProduct
    heat exchanger:   TubeSideFeed / TubeSideProduct / ShellSideFeed / ShellSideProduct
    component splitter: Inlets.Add / OverheadOutlets.Add / BottomOutlets
    fired heater (dynamicfiredheaterop): RadInlet.Add / RadOutlet.Add (process tube passes,
        several accepted), CombustionProduct (flue gas), BurnerFeed (fuel; single-valued)
    column: Operations.Add(name, type) + LoadColumnTemplate (done by add_unit); the tray
        section is ColumnFlowsheet.Operations "Main Tower" (TypeName traysection, 10 stages);
        tray feeds: MainTower.AddFeedStream(name, stage, False) exports a main-flowsheet
        stream of that name; top/bottom feeds: col.TopLiquidFeed / col.BtmVapourFeed;
        products: col.TopVapourProduct / TopLiquidProduct / TopWaterProduct (3-phase
        condenser) / BtmLiquidProduct; side draws: MainTower.AddDrawStream(name, phase,
        stage, True, False) with phase 1 liquid / 0 vapour / 2 water.
        Not usable on V11: SpecifyFeedLocation / SpecifyDrawLocation with main-flowsheet
        streams, ColumnFlowsheet.FeedStreams.Add, condenser/reboiler VapourProduct and
        LiquidProduct (do not exist), Condenser.LiquidStream (attaches only inside the
        column), connect() on a column.
    energy streams: flowsheet.EnergyStreams.Add(name); op.EnergyStream = <stream>
    The file head, Step 1 (HYSYS start-up), Step 3b and Step 7 are owned by the template
    and restored before every execution. Step 7 prints the summary, writes
    <case>_topology.json, saves the case and exits with code 2 when a listed connection
    could not be made, so the Fixer receives the exact error texts and can choose
    another role.

8. SELF-CHECK BEFORE ANSWERING
------------------------------
  [ ] every stream id of streams_in and streams_out of every unit appears exactly once in that
      unit's group; no unit or stream that is not in units_unit_view
  [ ] roles are spelled exactly: single_in single_out multi_in mixer_out tee_in tee_out
      vapour_out liquid_out light_liquid_out heavy_liquid_out pfr_out tube_in tube_out
      shell_in shell_out splitter_in splitter_overhead splitter_bottom fired_in fired_out
      flue_gas_out fuel_in | overhead_vapour distillate water_draw bottoms side_draw
      (a misspelt role is reported as a failure, never guessed)
  [ ] heavy_liquid_out / light_liquid_out only on separator_3phase; fired_in on every
      process feed of a fired heater; no second single_in / single_out on one unit
  [ ] columns use only connect_column_feed / connect_column_product; overhead_vapour, distillate,
      water_draw and bottoms at most once per column (a duplicate becomes a side_draw);
      no skip() on a column; connect_energy on every reactor
  [ ] every listed stream appears in a connect / connect_column_* / skip line; skip only with
      one of the three allowed reasons; no bare WARNING comments; the number of such lines
      (connect_energy lines not counted) equals the total number of streams_in +
      streams_out entries; no connection is left as a comment
  [ ] no connect() call targets a unit created with TYPES["column_*"]
  [ ] the answer is the Step 6 block alone, starting with its marker line and without the
      Step 7 marker
